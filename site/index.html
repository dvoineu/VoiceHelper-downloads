Frequently Asked Questions

How is VoiceHelper different from built-in macOS Dictation and Apple Intelligence?

VoiceHelper runs open whisper.cpp models on your GPU (Metal) up to Large v3 Turbo across 14 languages without Apple Intelligence regional restrictions, shows a non-focus-stealing HUD right at the text cursor, supports voice commands ("new line", "send it") and auto-stop after pause, and lets you switch to Deepgram streaming or OpenAI / Groq anytime.

Do I need a subscription, account, or API key?

No. "On this Mac (Whisper)" works completely offline and free after downloading a model in Settings. Cloud engines (Deepgram, OpenAI, Groq) are optional and use your own API key.

Why does VoiceHelper ask for Accessibility permission?

On macOS, any menu bar utility that locates the active text caret and inserts text into another application window (Terminal, IDE, browser) relies on the macOS Accessibility API. Even if Accessibility is not granted, you can still dictate and copy text manually from History.

What happens if I switch windows or click elsewhere while dictating?

VoiceHelper records the target app, window, and AX text field when dictation starts. If focus moves or Secure Input (a password field) becomes active, automatic insertion stops immediately so text is never typed into the wrong window. Your full transcript remains ready to copy or paste.

Are my voice recordings saved to disk?

By default, "Save dictations → Off (memory only)" is active: audio stays in RAM only during transcription and is discarded right after. If you opt into 24-hour or 7-day retention, files are encrypted with AES-GCM and the key stays exclusively in your macOS Keychain.
