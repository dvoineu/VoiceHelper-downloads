Why Frazia

Type at the speed of speech, in every app

Speaking is several times faster than typing. Frazia lets you do it wherever you write.

  • Works in any app

    Messengers, mail, browsers, Notes, VS Code, Terminal. Text goes where your cursor is, and the window you are typing in stays active.

  • Free and offline

    Whisper recognition runs on your Mac’s graphics chip. No account, no subscription and no internet after the model is downloaded.

  • Private by default

    Audio is kept in memory only while it is being recognized, then discarded. Saving recordings in History is up to you.

  • Accurate in 14 languages

    The Large v3 Turbo model handles fast speech, terms and names. Choose a language or leave Auto-detect on.

  • Never types into the wrong place

    If you switch windows or a password field becomes active, Frazia doesn’t insert and keeps your text ready to paste.

  • Voice commands

    Say “new line” or “new paragraph” to format, or end with “send it” to insert the text and press Return.

Get started

Set up in four steps

About five minutes, most of it the model download. On first launch, a setup window walks you through the same steps.

  1. Install the app

    Download the DMG, open it and drag Frazia into Applications. Launch it: the Frazia icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen and the setup window opens.

    Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

    Applications

  2. Allow two permissions

    macOS asks for access once. Click Allow, then turn on the switch next to Frazia in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

    Then check the microphone: in Microphone keep Auto and say a few words. The level bar should reach the green zone.

    • MicrophoneTo hear your speech. Allowed
    • AccessibilityTo insert text where the cursor is. Allowed

  3. Choose the recommended model

    In Recognition keep the engine On this Mac and pick your dictation language. Download the model marked Recommended for your Mac: Frazia chooses it from your chip and memory.

    Medium and Tiny are also available. You can change the model at any time in Settings → Recognition; downloaded models are kept when you update.

  4. Press ⌥ Space and speak

    Put the cursor in any text field and press ⌥ Option + Space. A small recording window with a wave and a timer appears next to the cursor. Speak, then press ⌥ Space again: the text is inserted.

    Setup ends with a trial field, so you can check everything before using other apps. To change the shortcut, open Settings → Shortcuts, click Change and press the new combination.

Cheat sheet

Shortcuts and voice commands

The default mode is “Tap or hold”: one shortcut works two ways.

Keys

Tap ⌥ Space
Start recording; tap again to stop and insert
Hold ⌥ Space
Record while held; release to insert
⇧ Shift + stop
Insert and press Return, so a message is sent right away
⌥ ⇧ V
Paste the last dictation again
Esc
Cancel the recording

Voice commands

“new line”
Line break
“new paragraph”
Empty line between paragraphs
“send it” at the end
Insert and press Return

Commands work in English and Russian and only as a separate phrase, so “add a new line to the table” stays plain text. You can turn them off in Settings.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? Do I need an account or an API key?

Yes, it is free, and you need neither. The “On this Mac” engine works offline after you download a model. Cloud engines are optional and use your own API key.

Why does Frazia need Accessibility?

macOS only lets an app find the text cursor and insert text into another app’s window through Accessibility. Without it you can still dictate and copy the text from History yourself.

The text didn’t appear after dictation. What should I do?

First check that Frazia is turned on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If you switched windows or a password field was active, Frazia deliberately doesn’t insert: press ⌥ Shift V to paste the last dictation, or copy it from History in the menu bar.

Which model should I choose?

The one with the “Recommended for your Mac” badge. On Apple Silicon with 16 GB of memory or more it is Large v3 Turbo, the most accurate. With less memory it is Small for Russian and other languages, or Base for English.

Are my voice recordings saved?

Not by default. “Save dictations” is set to “Off — memory only”: audio stays in memory while it is being recognized and is then discarded. If you choose to keep recordings for 24 hours or 7 days, they are encrypted, and the key stays in your Mac’s Keychain.

Can I use a cloud service instead?

Yes, if you prefer. Deepgram shows text while you are still speaking; OpenAI and Groq recognize after you stop. You use your own API key, stored in the macOS Keychain, and the service may charge separately.

How is it different from the built-in macOS Dictation?

Frazia runs open Whisper models up to Large v3 Turbo on your Mac in 14 languages, shows its window next to the cursor without taking focus, understands voice commands and sending with Shift, and lets you switch to a cloud engine at any time.

How do I update or remove Frazia?

To update, quit Frazia and replace the app in Applications with the new version: settings, API keys and models are kept. There is no automatic updater yet. To remove it, quit the app and move it to the Trash.
